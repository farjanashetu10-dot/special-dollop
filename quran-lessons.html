FAQ

Common Questions

How does the free trial work? +
Your first lesson is completely free and has no obligation. It's a chance for us to meet, assess your level, and plan a personalised learning path. After that, you decide if you'd like to continue.
What platform do you use for lessons? +
All lessons are conducted over Zoom. You'll receive a personalized Zoom link by email after your booking is confirmed. No special equipment needed — just a device with a camera and microphone.
Can I book for my child? +
Absolutely! The Kids Special Program is specifically designed for children ages 5–14 with age-appropriate methods, stories, and games. Parents are welcome to sit in on lessons.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule? +
No problem — just give 24 hours notice via email or WhatsApp and we'll reschedule at no cost. Cancellations within 24 hours of the lesson may be charged.
What languages are lessons taught in? +
Lessons are available in English, Arabic, and Urdu. Just mention your preference in the booking form.
How much do lessons cost? +
30-minute lessons start from $15 and 60-minute lessons from $25. Monthly packages are available at a discounted rate. Payment is accepted via bank transfer or PayPal.
